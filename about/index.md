I play Go, and it was AlphaGo that first got me interested in AI. That interest eventually led me to psychology, and most of what I do now is use computational tools to study how people interact.

## Outside work

**Go.** Amateur 4‑dan. I play in local and online tournaments when I get the chance.

**Video games.** RPGs, action, simulation and sports games, on whatever platform is around. One day I'd like to make a game built on psychological research that is actually good for the people who play it.

**Football.** I support Chelsea. On the pitch I play fullback or centre-back, though these days I mostly play EA FC instead.

## Languages

Mandarin is my first language and I work in English. I'm a beginner in French (je ne parle pas bien le français) and Japanese (日本語が下手です).
