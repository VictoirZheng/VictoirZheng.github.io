About Me

It can be tricky for non-Mandarin speakers, so here is a quick guide to my name, Qingguang Zheng (郑庆广).

My family name, Zheng, sounds like JUNG. My given name, Qingguang, has two syllables: CHING + GWAHNG.

“Qing” rhymes with “sing” or “king”. “Guang” is “gwa” plus the “-ng” in “song”.

All together: CHING-GWAHNG JUNG. I am also happy to go by my English name, Victor.

Victoir is my online handle, used for this site and my GitHub. In person, Victor works fine.

Research

I study what happens when couples talk: the topics they raise, the words they use (for example “we” versus “I”), and how their language, facial expressions, gaze and even brain activity fall into or out of sync. I then ask how these patterns predict marital quality and well-being over time.

Coding hours of conversation by hand is slow and hard to keep consistent. With carefully designed few-shot prompts, LLMs reached 94% agreement with our coding scheme (from 67% zero-shot), on par with trained annotators. That makes it practical to analyze much larger and richer interaction datasets, with humans still validating the results.
