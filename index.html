Qingguang “Victor” Zheng 郑庆广

Ecological Psychology · Language & Cognition · Relationship Science

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Psychological Sciences at the University of Connecticut, training in the Ecological Psychology and Language & Cognition tracks.

I study how people coordinate with each other in conversation: what couples and families talk about, how they talk, and how their language, emotion, behavior and physiology fall into and out of sync, and how these dynamics shape relationships and well-being. My work combines dyadic observation with natural language processing, large language models, time-series and synchrony analysis, and fNIRS hyperscanning.

Before UConn, I earned my B.Sc. (2023) and M.Ed. (2026) in psychology at Beijing Normal University, advised by Prof. Xiaoyi Fang.
