I'm a Ph.D. student in Psychological Sciences at the University of Connecticut, in the Ecological Psychology and Language & Cognition tracks.

I'm interested in conversation as something two people do together. When a couple talks, their words, expressions and timing start to line up, and I want to know what that coordination says about the relationship. Before UConn I spent seven years at Beijing Normal University, mostly studying couples and families, and four of them also working on a national study of student mental health.

Outside work I play Go (amateur 4‑dan), follow Chelsea, and unwind with video games.
