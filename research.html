Research

I use computational methods to understand close relationships: what couples talk about, how they talk, and how their emotions, behavior and brains synchronize during interaction. A parallel line of work applies large-scale data analysis to the mental health of Chinese students.

Research Projects

The Effect of Marital Interaction on Marital Relationship: Behavioral and Cognitive Neural Mechanisms

NSFC General Program · 2022–present · PI: Prof. Xiaoyi Fang · Primary student researcher

  • Applied LIWC, LDA topic modeling and BERT fine-tuning to 720 spousal interaction transcripts.
  • Designed a few-shot prompting strategy for LLMs (Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen) to automate thematic coding, raising consistency from a 67% zero-shot baseline to 94%, comparable to human annotators.
  • Built a multimodal pipeline (OpenFace facial AU tracking, Whisper speech-to-text, LLM sentiment) for couple interaction videos.
  • Extracted language, facial-expression and gaze synchrony with windowed cross-correlation, recurrence quantification and detrended fluctuation analysis.

Analyses of Chinese College Students’ Mental Health and Revision of the College Students Mental Health Screening Scale (CSMHSS)

Ministry of Education · 2022–present · PI: Prof. Xiaoyi Fang · Primary student researcher

  • Built a reproducible R/Python pipeline (7,000+ lines) automating cleaning, modeling, visualization, scale revision and report generation.
  • Evaluate reliability, validity and norms of the CSMHSS each year with samples of 250,000+ students.
  • Analyze nationwide prevalence, regional distribution and trends of mental health problems among 5,000,000+ students per year.
  • Lead a four-person graduate team producing annual evidence-based reports for national policy.

The Dynamic Association between Marital Interaction and Marital Relationship among Newlyweds: The Mediating Role of Marital Emotion and Affection

NSFC General Program · 2020–2023 · PI: Prof. Xiaoyi Fang · Primary student researcher

  • Ran fNIRS hyperscanning experiments with 50+ couples on a NIRScout system (150+ hours).
  • Managed a database of 2,000+ interaction recordings (1,000+ hours, 4 TB): quality control, metadata tagging and transcoding.
  • Built an automated pipeline producing personalized feedback reports for 400+ participating couples.
  • Led a five-person team to compile 100+ interaction video stimuli and collect 20,000+ external valence ratings.

Emotional Semantic Space: Individual Differences and Prediction of Emotional Well-Being

National Undergraduate Innovation Program · 2021–2022 · PI: Prof. Yanchao Bi · Core team member

  • Ran the Multiple Arrangement Task (MATLAB) to map individual lexical-semantic spaces.
  • Programmed Go/No-Go and word-association paradigms in PsychoPy and deployed them online.
  • Predicted depression and anxiety from semantic-space metrics (N = 275) with hierarchical clustering and relevance vector regression.
Semantic SpaceRSARVRPsychoPyMental Health

Associations Between Individual Lexical-Semantic Spaces and Emotional Patterns, Social Values and Moral Tendencies

School-level Undergraduate Innovation Program · 2020–2021 · PI: Prof. Yanchao Bi · Core team member

  • Measured lexical-semantic structure with paired-similarity ratings and triplet odd-one-out tasks.
  • Captured emotion-recognition dynamics with MouseTracker.
  • Linked semantic structure to mouse trajectories, social value orientation and moral judgment with SVM and RSA.
