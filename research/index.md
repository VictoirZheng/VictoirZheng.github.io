  quantification analysis and detrended fluctuation analysis; for video and audio, OpenFace and Whisper.
  I have also run fNIRS hyperscanning studies and worked with fMRI data.

My research is about how people coordinate when they talk, and what that coordination means for their relationships and well-being. At UConn I'm coming at these questions from ecological psychology and from the study of language and cognition.

The projects below are from Beijing Normal University, where I worked with Prof. Xiaoyi Fang and, as an undergraduate, with Prof. Yanchao Bi.
